# Use standing personal-data and maintenance authorizations

On 2026-10-14 the user confirmed the complete Q1–Q11 design in [private-data authority](../private-data-authority.md): Study/Life source roots and future files inherit approved read/model/owner-channel permissions, approved providers may use relevant minimal context, and existing-project maintenance may proceed within a standing scope. This replaces ADR 0032's current-file-only limit and the earlier blanket private-channel/remote-control restrictions because a personal assistant needs continuing access to the user's private records without repeating the same permission request.

Credentials and explicit exclusions remain unavailable; new roots/providers, higher budgets, deleting originals and external writes beyond approved owner replies/notifications require their own scope. Source authority remains on Mac, owner identity must be verified at every authorization entry point, tentative memory remains distinguishable from self-reports, and revocation/retention affect derived data; authorization does not imply that the corresponding service capability has been implemented or that host-enforced approval rules can be overridden.
