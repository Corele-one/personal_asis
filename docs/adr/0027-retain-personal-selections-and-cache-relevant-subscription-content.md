# Retain personal selections and cache relevant subscription content

Personal Selections remain searchable with best-effort backfill of all accessible history, while subscription entries are mainly collected incrementally after activation and their relevant available bodies are cached for 30 days unless selected for longer retention. This preserves selected reading evidence while bounding feed-driven storage, processing, and model costs; coverage gaps remain explicit, author text and user annotations remain distinct, and source systems remain authoritative. Cached bodies may be promoted automatically under approved rules as specified in ADR 0030; exact rules, expiry mechanics, selected-content deletion, source/provider policy, and minimal citation retention still need configuration, and local retention does not write favorites back to platforms.
